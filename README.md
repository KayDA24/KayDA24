Hi, I'm Dokyun Kwon, an undergraduate researcher at Sungkyunkwan University. My research interests lie in multimodal learning, robot learning, and world models. I am particularly interested in bridging the gap between future prediction and reliable decision-making in robots. My long-term goal is to develop world models that enable robots to anticipate the consequences of their actions, account for uncertainty, and adapt their plans to unexpected changes in dynamic, real-world environments.

**[URP] RuleCF-AD: Rule-Guided Counterfactual Learning for Logical Anomaly Detection** · Ongoing

Developing a multimodal framework that combines natural-language rules and counterfactual image interventions to improve logical anomaly detection and the faithfulness of model decisions.

**[Industry–Academia Collaboration] Used Vehicle Damage Classification and Detection (CHEXCAR)** · Completed
